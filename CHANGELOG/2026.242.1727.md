# 2026.242.1727 — feat(WP5): gate the wl: verb against the nested compositor

## Summary

Brings the `wl:` verb's wlroots tooling into the corpus and authors the **first checks to exercise
that verb against the nested Hyprland**.

Before this, `wl-clipboard`, `wtype` and `wlr-randr` were simply absent from the image — only `grim`
was there — so `wl: clipboard`, `type`, `key-combo` and `resolution` failed on **`command not
found`** rather than on anything about the desktop. That is why none of them had ever been gated
here.

```
layer-cstream-desktop  v2026.242.1606 -> v2026.242.1712
```

| new check | what it catches |
|---|---|
| `wl-clipboard-set` | the verb cannot reach `wl-copy` at all |
| `wl-clipboard-roundtrip` | a clipboard that accepts a write and loses it |
| `wl-screenshot-content` | screencopy denied, or a black/uniform frame |

The clipboard is a **set→get round-trip with a canary**, because a `set` alone passes happily
against a clipboard that silently swallows writes. The screenshot asserts `artifact_not_uniform`
and is the **first live proof that Hyprland's `enforce_permissions = true` plus the `hl.permission`
grant for `grim` actually permits screencopy** — the plan flags that as a trap where every capture
is otherwise denied.

The bed goes from **22 checks to 25**.

## ⛔ Deliberately NOT gated: `wl: resolution`

Shipping `wlr-randr` makes that verb *functional* here, and measured on this bed it is **harmful**:

```
WP4 resize 640 480       ->  streamer 640x480,  compositor 640x480    (coherent)
wl: resolution 1280x720  ->  compositor 1280x720                      (wlr-randr succeeds)
streamer `geometry`      ->  STILL 640x480                            (never told)
```

The compositor renders 1280×720 while the encoder is configured for 640×480, **with no error
anywhere**. `wlr-randr` is a second writer to a property the cstream control socket owns.

Tracked as **`opencharly/pod-cstream#11`** with the measurement, a reproduction and three costed
options. The reason is written **inline in the bed**, not only here, so the next reader does not
"fix" the omission. Resize on a cstream desktop goes through the control socket — the
`cstream-resize-*` checks, which assert the compositor's reported geometry.
