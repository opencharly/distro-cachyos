# 2026.246.0100 — feat: OpenClaw 2.0 boxes + plugin-openclaw verb in the beds

## Summary

Advance the openclaw box family to **OpenClaw 2.0** and wire the new
`openclaw:` check verb into the beds:

- **box/openclaw** — `pod-openclaw` pin `v2026.239.1639` → `v2026.246.1617`
  (npm `openclaw@2026.8.2`, the 2.0 tag) and compose
  `plugin-openclaw/candy/plugin-openclaw:v2026.246.1626` (the new `openclaw:`
  check verb).
- **box/openclaw-full** and **box/openclaw-desktop** — `layer-openclaw-full` pin
  `v2026.240.0119` → `v2026.246.1625` (the tag carrying the 2.0 gateway).
- **check-openclaw-pod** — new plan steps authoring the `openclaw:` verb against
  the running 2.0 gateway: `health` (`/healthz`), `ready` (`/readyz`), and
  `models` (in-venue CLI) — the R10 proof for plugin-openclaw.
- **check-openclaw-full-pod** and **check-openclaw-desktop-pod** — an
  `openclaw: health` step each.

The gateway startup surface is unchanged in 2.0 (the supervised exec line, the
`gateway.mode=local` guard, and the `~/.openclaw/openclaw.json` config carry
over), so the service/port/volume wiring is untouched.
