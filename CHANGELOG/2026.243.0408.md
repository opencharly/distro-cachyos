## Why this chain took five PRs instead of one

Worth recording, because the shape recurs. `pod-hyprland` was pinned in **two** places —
`layer-cstream-desktop` and `pod-cstream` — and two pins on one candy report
`resolved to multiple versions` every time either moves. No ordering of cross-repo PRs avoids that,
because they cannot change atomically.

The fix was not a better ordering but removing the duplicate: `pod-cstream#15` deleted its
`pod-hyprland` require on the grounds the layer's own description already states — a metalayer's
members *are what it is*, and installing the same thing in two places gives "a consumer two places
to look for the same behaviour". The layer is now the **sole owner** of that pin, so no future
`pod-hyprland` bump can reproduce the skew.
